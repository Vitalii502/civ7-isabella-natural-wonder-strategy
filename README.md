# Civ VII Isabella Natural Wonder Snowball Strategy

A Deity-level Civilization VII strategy built around Natural Wonder scaling, Chola trade, religion, Influence, and Culture.

## Core setup

**Leader:** Isabella  
**Antiquity:** Iceland  
**Exploration:** Chola  
**Modern:** Chola  

**Map:** Huge  
**Difficulty:** Deity  
**Opponents:** 12 civilizations

## The basic idea

The strategy is built around stacking several systems that reinforce each other:

1. **Use Iceland and Isabella to find and control Natural Wonders early.**
2. **Capture as many Natural Wonders as possible.**
3. Isabella gives **+50% Natural Wonder tile yields for every Natural Wonder you control**, so every new wonder strengthens all the previous ones.
4. Use Natural Wonder mementos to add additional yield types to those already-scaled tiles.
5. In Exploration, switch to **Chola** and build a large trade network.
6. Spread your religion across the map using:
   - **Dawah**
   - **Missionaries**
   - **Convert Population** whenever it comes off cooldown
7. Take **Tikkun Olam** so foreign settlements following your religion generate Influence.
8. Complete **Toshakhana** so the Founder Belief continues into Modern.
9. Build and preserve Influence infrastructure such as **Guildhalls** instead of replacing everything at the Age transition.
10. Stay **Chola in Modern** so the trade network continues generating large amounts of Influence.
11. Build **Chengde Mountain Resort** once you have Trade Routes with many civilizations. Its empire-wide Culture multiplier becomes enormous once your base Culture is already in the thousands.

## Why the strategy snowballs

The key interaction is Isabella's Natural Wonder scaling.

If you control 7 Natural Wonders:

**7 × 50% = +350% Natural Wonder tile yields**

That multiplier applies across every Natural Wonder you own.

So acquiring another Natural Wonder does two things at once:

- gives you another powerful wonder
- increases the yields of every Natural Wonder you already control

This creates extremely fast scaling on a Huge map.

## Religion + Influence engine

In Exploration, the Chola trade network becomes a second snowball.

**Dawah** converts settlements through Trade Routes.

**Missionaries** convert settlements manually.

**Convert Population** lets you use Influence to convert additional foreign settlements without needing to physically move Missionaries there.

With **Tikkun Olam**, those converted settlements then generate Influence:

- +2 Influence per foreign settlement following your religion
- +1 Influence per domestic settlement following your religion

Then Toshakhana allows this Founder Belief to continue into Modern.

## Chola in Modern

Staying Chola into Modern keeps the trade engine alive.

In this run there were about **20 active Trade Routes**.

Modern Chola generated roughly:

**20 Trade Routes × 3 Influence = +60 Influence per turn**

This stacked with:

- Tikkun Olam
- preserved Influence buildings
- Modern Influence buildings
- other diplomatic bonuses

Final Influence output reached roughly:

**+372 Influence per turn**

## Culture finisher: Chengde Mountain Resort

The main Modern Culture multiplier was **Chengde Mountain Resort**.

Its effect gives:

**+5% Culture for every other civilization with which you have a Trade Route**

With Trade Routes to 12 other civilizations:

**12 × 5% = +60% empire-wide Culture**

At the end of the run, total Culture was:

**+5,274 Culture per turn**

Without the +60% modifier, the empire was producing roughly:

**5,274 / 1.60 ≈ 3,296 Culture**

So Chengde contributed approximately:

**+1,978 Culture per turn**

from a single Wonder.

## Example Natural Wonder tile

One scaled Natural Wonder tile reached approximately:

- **54 Production**
- **36 Gold**
- **66 Culture**
- **42 Happiness**

Those yields were repeated across multiple tiles of the same Natural Wonder.

## Final result

The game ended with a Culture Victory on approximately **Modern Turn 34**.

Final empire yields were roughly:

- **+2,917 Gold**
- **+1,705 Science**
- **+5,274 Culture**
- **+2,783 Happiness**
- **+372 Influence**
- **20 / 28 settlements**

This was achieved on **Deity difficulty** on a **Huge map** against **12 other civilizations**.

## Strategy summary

**Iceland discovers the map.  
Isabella turns Natural Wonders into scaling super-tiles.  
Chola turns trade into Influence.  
Dawah + Missionaries + Convert Population spread religion across the world.  
Tikkun Olam converts that religious control into even more Influence.  
Toshakhana carries the religious engine into Modern.  
Preserved Influence buildings add another permanent layer.  
Chengde multiplies the already enormous Culture base by 60%.**

The result is a very fast late-game snowball where Culture, Influence, Gold, Happiness, Science, and Production all scale together.
